# Temüjin & Borte

*The things you will do for a woman you love.*

An epic of Temüjin (Genghis Khan) and Borte: the betrothal on the steppe, the Merkit raid, Jochi "the guest", Borte's ransom, the anda Jamukha, the conquests, the gates of Vienna, and Kublai Khan, ending with the scribe's envoi.

**Read it:** https://rohanshiloh.github.io/genghis/

Co-authors: Shiloh & Rohan
